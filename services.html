Residential Home Loans

Buying a home is one of the most important decisions of your life. We support first-home buyers accessing NSW stamp duty exemptions and the 5% First Home Guarantee, as well as upgraders and downsizers across Sydney.

  • Borrowing capacity maximization
  • Fast pre-approval in 24–48 hours
  • Fixed, variable & split loan strategies

Mortgage Refinancing

Is your current loan still working for you? Rates and personal circumstances change. Our free 7-point home loan health check compares your current rate against wholesale market alternatives to see if switching can save you thousands.

  • Rate reduction & repayment relief
  • Equity cash-out for renovations
  • Debt consolidation into one payment

Investment Property Loans

Building wealth through real estate requires careful debt structuring. We work alongside your accountant to ensure loan structures enhance cash flow, maximize tax deductibility, and allow rapid portfolio growth.

  • Interest-only & 100% offset accounts
  • Standalone security (no cross-collateralization)
  • Usable equity release for deposits

SMSF Property Loans

Gain more control over your superannuation. We specialize in Limited Recourse Borrowing Arrangements (LRBA) for purchasing commercial office suites, warehouses, or residential investment property inside your SMSF.

  • Compliant bare trust & LRBA setups
  • Commercial property (business premises lease)
  • Specialist SMSF bank & non-bank lenders

Commercial & Business Loans

Fuel your enterprise growth. We arrange commercial mortgages, working capital facilities, fit-out finance, and professional practice funding tailored for medical practitioners, legal professionals, and company founders.

Car & Asset Finance
