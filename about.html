Transparency & Fiduciary Care

How Our Business Works & How We Are Paid

Clients frequently ask why our initial review and loan strategy sessions are 100% free of charge. Here is the straightforward answer in plain English:

The Australian Best Interests Duty (BID)

Under Australian law, licensed mortgage brokers are legally bound to act in your best interests when recommending a credit product. Unlike bank branch lenders who can only sell their own products, we compare over 70 lenders to find the optimal match for you.

When your loan settles, the chosen lender pays our brokerage an upfront and ongoing commission for managing the application and ongoing service. These commissions are fully disclosed in writing in your Credit Proposal before you proceed. You pay nothing to us for our standard residential broking services.
