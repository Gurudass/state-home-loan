Calculator Assumptions & Regulatory Disclaimer

1. Indicative Estimates Only: Results generated by these calculators are illustrative estimates and do not constitute an offer of credit, formal quote, or pre-approval under the National Consumer Credit Protection Act 2009 (NCCP).

2. APRA Serviceability Buffer: In compliance with Australian Prudential Regulation Authority guidance, borrowing capacity estimates apply a minimum 3.00% serviceability buffer above the contract interest rate.

3. Income & Expenses: Calculations estimate net income using current Australian individual tax brackets (including Stage 3 reforms and Medicare levy). Living expenses are benchmarked using Household Expenditure Measure (HEM) standards.

4. Stamp Duty & Government Charges: Transfer duty figures reflect current state rates, including the NSW First Home Buyers Assistance Scheme (FHBAS). Does not include legal conveyancing disbursements or council adjustment rates.

5. Licensing: State Home Loan Centre brokers are credit representatives bound by Australian Best Interests Duty (BID). Credit criteria, fees, charges, and lending terms apply.
