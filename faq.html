Frequently Asked Questions

  • Does using State Home Loan Centre cost me any fees?

    For standard residential home loans, refinancing, and investment property purchases, our mortgage advisory service is completely free to you. We are remunerated by the lender you select upon loan settlement. All commissions are transparently disclosed in our Credit Proposal Disclosure prior to lodgement.

  • What is the Best Interests Duty (BID) and why does it matter?

    Under Australian law, licensed mortgage brokers are legally required to act in your best interests when recommending credit products. In contrast, retail branch lenders are not bound by BID—they can only recommend their employer's products, even if a competitor offers a cheaper rate or better features.

  • How many lenders do you compare for my scenario?

    We hold active accreditations with over 70 Australian lenders. This includes the Big 4 banks (CBA, Westpac, ANZ, NAB), regional lenders (Macquarie, Bank of Queensland, Bendigo, ING, Suncorp), and specialist non-bank institutions for complex, self-employed, or SMSF scenarios.

  • How much deposit do I actually need to buy a home in NSW?

    While a 20% deposit avoids Lenders Mortgage Insurance (LMI), eligible first home buyers can purchase with as little as a 5% deposit through the Australian Government's First Home Guarantee scheme with no LMI payable. We also assist clients with guarantor structures allowing 100% financing.

  • How long does formal loan pre-approval take in Sydney?

    Lender turnaround times vary from 48 hours for fast-tracked digital lenders to 5–7 business days for major institutions during peak periods. As your broker, we actively track daily SLAs across all lenders to recommend options matching your settlement or auction timeline.

  • Can I get a loan if I am self-employed or have complex tax structures?

    Absolutely. We specialize in self-employed scenarios, family trust distributions, company directors, and sole traders. We work directly with your accountant and utilize Alt-Doc or BAS-based income verification policies with specialized Australian lenders.

  • What ongoing support do you provide after my loan settles?

    Our client relationship does not end at settlement. We perform proactive annual loan health checks, repricing requests with your lender when RBA cash rate shifts occur, and support you when you are ready to upgrade, renovate, or invest.
